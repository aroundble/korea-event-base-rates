Method

방법론

  • Source: KRX KIND disclosure feed with minute timestamps; corrected/duplicate filings removed.
  • Disclosure types are title families classified from the filing title (e.g. 전환사채 → convertible bond, 자기주식취득 → buyback); a family includes related follow-up filings (e.g. results reports, listing notices), identically in the history and the daily feed.
  • Entry: before 09:00 → open of the same day; during session → close of the day; after 15:20 → next open.
  • Returns: to the close of entry day +1, +5, +20 trading days, minus the KOSPI/KOSDAQ index return over the same window, net of Korean trading costs.
  • Liquidity buckets by 20-day average traded value before the disclosure.
  • Robust ●: the sample is split in half by date; both halves must show the same sign with |t|>2.
  • Daily feed: recent filings from the KIND daily disclosure list (DART as fallback), matched to the base rate of the same type × timing × liquidity (falling back to coarser groups when fewer than 30 past events). Refreshed after the Korean close on trading days.
  • Limitations: ~5 years of data, overlapping events, survivorship not fully controlled, classification by title can misfile some filings.
  • No raw price data is published — only derived statistics.
